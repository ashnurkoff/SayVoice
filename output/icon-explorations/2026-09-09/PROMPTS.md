## Generation prompts

### Glass Voice

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: a polished macOS application icon concept for SayVoice, a private on-device voice dictation app.
Primary request: create one exceptional, modern, highly legible app icon. Contemporary Apple-like material refinement with an original identity, not a copy of an existing app.
Subject: a single bold sculptural microphone symbol, made from luminous solid blue-violet optical glass. A rounded upright capsule suspended within a thick simple U-shaped cradle, a very short stem and understated base. The capsule has only one subtle vertical inset, no grille pattern. Every curve is carefully proportioned. The silhouette is recognizable instantly at small size.
Scene/backdrop: the microphone sits centered on a soft pearl-white rounded-square macOS icon tile with generous continuous corners. The tile occupies 84 percent of a square image; evenly spaced margin outside is a very pale neutral gray. Soft shallow contact shadow. Perfectly straight-on orthographic view.
Style/medium: exquisitely art-directed 3D icon render, restrained depth, satin optical glass, broad soft edge highlights, no busy refractions. Professional and calm.
Color palette: saturated periwinkle and deep iris, a small cool cyan highlight on the upper edge, warm pearl background. The microphone is mostly solid and high contrast, not transparent enough to disappear.
Composition/framing: one icon only, centered, emblem about 56 percent of tile height with generous breathing room. Render as a square high-resolution image.
Text: none.
Avoid: letters, words, app name, watermark, multiple icons, collage, presentation labels, desk scene, perspective tilt, sound-wave arcs, sparkle stars, rainbow effects, tiny details, photorealistic recording equipment, heavy shadows.
```

### Speech Cut

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: a premium macOS application icon concept for SayVoice, a voice-to-text app.
Primary request: design a distinctively bold, timeless app icon with the clarity and friendly geometric confidence of modern Google product design, while retaining an original brand identity.
Subject: one large warm-white speech-bubble emblem. Its silhouette is a horizontally rounded soft rectangle with a compact carefully integrated lower-left tail. Three vertical rounded slots are cut out of the white speech bubble, exposing the colored tile behind: short, tall, medium height, aligned around a common horizontal center, evoking a voice waveform. This is one integrated mark, not separate symbols. Broad geometries and optically balanced spacing make it instantly legible at small sizes.
Scene/backdrop: a vibrant warm vermilion rounded-square macOS icon tile with large continuous corners. Very subtle tonal transition from coral-red upper left to red-orange lower right. Tile occupies 84 percent of a square canvas, surrounded by an even very pale neutral gray margin.
Style/medium: rigorous minimal geometric brand icon, nearly flat, a tactile matte enamel surface with only the slightest raised relief and soft local shadow on the white emblem. Expensive editorial simplicity. Straight-on orthographic view.
Composition/framing: one icon only, centered emblem about 62 percent of tile width. Generous breathing room. Square high-resolution image.
Text: none.
Avoid: words, letters, watermark, multiple icons, collage, presentation labels, Google logo, exact Google four-color palette, microphone equipment, decorative sparkles, glossy plastic, neon glow, excessive gradients, tiny details, thick outlines, perspective tilt.
```

### Silver Flow

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: a distinctive premium macOS app icon concept for SayVoice, an on-device voice dictation utility.
Primary request: create a sculptural monogram that feels like a beautifully designed native Mac tool, sophisticated, modern and memorable.
Subject: a single continuous broad metallic ribbon curved into a fluid upright S silhouette. The S also subtly suggests the smooth rise and fall of a voice waveform. It has two clean open bends, strong negative spaces, thick sculptural sections, rounded ends. Simple silhouette first, graceful material second. It should clearly read as an S, not an infinity symbol, chain link or snake.
Scene/backdrop: a deep near-black graphite rounded-square tile with large continuous corners. Tile occupies 84 percent of the square canvas. Outside margin a very pale neutral gray with one restrained shallow contact shadow. No scenery. Straight-on orthographic front view.
Style/medium: art-directed 3D brand icon, satin silver titanium with a delicate icy lavender reflection along one edge. Broad large smooth reflections, controlled highlights, refined shallow sculptural depth. Original identity with contemporary Apple-like industrial material quality.
Composition/framing: one centered icon only, ribbon emblem around 61 percent of tile height, balanced breathing room, square high-resolution output. High contrast so the overall shape holds at small size.
Text: no text beyond the abstract S-shaped symbol itself.
Avoid: words, app name, watermark, multiple icons, collage, presentation labels, sound bars, literal microphone, stars, glitter, complex reflections, liquid splashes, drips, chrome noise, harsh lens flare, perspective tilt, exaggerated extrusion, gaming aesthetics.
```
