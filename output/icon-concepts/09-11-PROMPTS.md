## 09-vermilion-fold

Output: `09-vermilion-fold.png`

Use case: logo-brand. Create one exceptionally art-directed macOS application icon concept for SayVoice, an on-device voice-to-text app. The user rejected ordinary microphones, speech bubbles, waveform bars and an S ribbon as banal. This new design must feel like a sophisticated independent design studio created an original visual identity. One square image, one icon, no contact sheet. Strong large silhouette, disciplined geometry, beautiful negative space. Straight-on, no perspective. One rounded-square tile occupying 84% of image on a perfectly uniform opaque warm off-white backdrop. Absolutely no microphone, speech bubble, equalizer bars, alphabet letters, S, ribbons, AI sparkles, neon glow, rainbow gradients, stock symbols, text, captions, watermark. Direction: VERMILION / FOLD. A striking vivid vermilion-orange matte tile. At center an original sculptural ink-black mark representing a single opening or folded sound aperture: two broad, smooth opposing crescent-like planes, one above and one below, with a single beautiful diagonal negative-space slit between them. Together they suggest an abstract compact circular form in gentle rotation, subtly like a mouth opening, but never a literal mouth or eyes. The upper plane curves toward the viewer, lower plane curves away. Slight physical relief, extremely controlled dark-on-dark shading, no glossy toy rendering. Only two interlocking shapes, broad powerful masses, precise curves and generous separation. The mark occupies 57 percent of tile width. Swiss graphic discipline meets expressive contemporary industrial design. Warm vermilion and near black only, understated material quality, icon designed to work as a one-color stamp too.
