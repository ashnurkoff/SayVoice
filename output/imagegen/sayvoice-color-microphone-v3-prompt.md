## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: SayVoice macOS dictation application icon, square PNG master 1024x1024.
Art direction: a beautifully proportioned contemporary 2D app logo with the confident colorful geometric simplicity of Google product identities and the Apple App Store glyph. ORIGINAL voice identity, do not copy their actual logos. This should look like an expertly art-directed shipping app icon, not clip art, not a product render.
Subject: ONE compact microphone emblem constructed from three generously thick, perfectly smooth graphic shapes. An upright short rounded capsule in vivid azure blue, a thick open U-shaped cradle surrounding its lower half in rich violet, and a short centered stem seamlessly flowing into a small rounded horizontal foot in the same violet. The capsule is substantial, not thin. The open cradle has wide clean dark negative space around the capsule. Rounded geometry, optical balance, very few shapes, recognizable immediately at 24px. The microphone is a purely abstract flat symbol; no grille, no hardware details. Use a restrained smooth azure-to-blue gradient on the capsule and violet-to-periwinkle gradient on the cradle, giving fresh luminous color while staying entirely 2D. Strong saturated colors with clear separation from the backdrop. No disconnected decorative elements.
Composition: precisely centered single emblem, about 60% canvas height and 48% canvas width, generous margins, front-facing flat graphic.
Background: flat solid dark #17171D over the entire square, perfectly uniform from edge to edge. Full square with sharp corners. User will apply squircle mask manually.
Strict constraints: no text, no letters, no frame, no enclosing rounded-square tile, no rounded-corner canvas mask, no speech bubble, no waveform bars, no additional symbols. No 3D, no extrusion, no metallic or glass surfaces, no photorealism, no texture, no bevel, no drop shadow, no cast shadow, no ambient glow. Gradients only inside the colored symbol, never on the background. Crisp edges, professional geometric polish.
